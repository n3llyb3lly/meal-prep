Copied rows are tab-separated, so they land in separate columns. Click the first empty cell in column A of the named sheet and paste. Then rebuild catalog.json above.
Then: upload catalog.json and catalog-v1.json to your meal-prep repository on GitHub, replacing the old ones. catalog.json (schema 2, with stores, offers and the item tree) is for version 6 phones; catalog-v1.json is for phones still on 5.3. Phones connected to the Google Sheet don't need either file. The app picks it up on its next launch with a connection (GitHub can take up to about 10 minutes to serve the new copy).
