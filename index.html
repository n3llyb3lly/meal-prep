Help and guide
Tap a question to open it. Tap it again to close it.
How does a trip work?

A trip runs from one Aldi visit to the next. The four steps at the top of Days show where you are:

  1. Menu: pick meals for the trip.
  2. Shop: on Groceries, tap I'm at the store and tick items into the cart.
  3. Receipt: log what you paid. This puts your food into the kitchen.
  4. Cook: use the Days tab each day until the next trip.

The big button under the steps always takes you to the next thing to do. Already at the store with no plan? Tap Start a trip, then I'm at Aldi now. Popping in for milk mid-week? When you log that receipt, choose Top-up for this trip.

Where do I start?

Follow these four steps the first time. After that, repeat them each time you shop.

  1. Pick your meals. Tap Menu at the bottom of the screen. Open Suggested and tap Add next to meals you like. If you'd rather not choose, tap Fill my trip for me.
  2. Go shopping. Tap Groceries. This is your shopping list. At Aldi, tap I'm at the store, then tap each item as it goes in your cart.
  3. Log your receipt. At home, tap Log receipt on the Groceries tab. This puts your food into the app's kitchen.
  4. Eat. Each day, tap Days to see what to make.
What does each button at the bottom do?
  • Days: what to eat today and tomorrow, and a short list of the rest of the trip.
  • Menu: suggested meals, every recipe, and the meals you picked for this trip.
  • Groceries: your shopping list, budget and receipts.
  • At home: two parts. Kitchen is the food you have, what you could make, your pantry and always-stocked items. Essentials are the non-food things you keep around, like foil and toothpaste.
  • Settings: this page. People, budget, shopping day, backups, prices and history.
How do I choose meals?
  1. Tap Menu. Suggested shows meals that fit what's left of your trip. Browse all shows every recipe.
  2. In Browse all, type in the search box to find a meal or an ingredient, such as "chicken". Tap Breakfasts, Lunches or Dinners to see only those.
  3. Each meal shows how much it adds to your shopping list. Meals that use food you already have cost less.
  4. Tap 2x to cook double and have leftovers for another day.
  5. Tap Add. The meal moves to My menu.

If the button says Over by, that meal would go past your budget. Tap it twice if you want it anyway.

Some days I have no time to cook. What do I do?

On the Days tab, tap the button under a day's name (it starts as Normal day) and choose Busy, Normal or Have time. Choose Busy on a rushed day. The app then suggests leftovers and quick meals first.

If your week has a pattern, such as busy weekdays, set it once in Settings › Household.

When picking meals, open Filters in Browse all and tap 15 min to see only quick recipes. Cooking 2x on a free day gives you leftovers for the busy ones.

My shopping day changes from week to week.

That's fine. After you log a receipt, the app asks When's your next trip? Tap a choice, or pick a date.

To change it later, tap Groceries, then Edit next to Trip, then Change the next trip date. You can also pick a new shopping day in Settings › Household. The days, the number of meals and the budget all follow the new date.

The budget for a shorter trip is a share of your weekly budget. You can type a different amount for one trip.

How do I add my Aldi receipt?
  1. Tap Groceries, then Log receipt. The items you ticked in the cart are already listed.
  2. Check each line. Use − + for how many you bought. Type either the price for one (after @) or the line total (after =); the other fills in. Tap + Add a line for anything you forgot to tick, and tap an item name for sizes, sales or to change the item.
  3. If the app asks Sale price or new regular price?, pick one. A sale doesn't change the usual price.
  4. Type the total printed on the receipt so the app can check the lines, then tap Add N items.

You can stop halfway. The app saves what you typed, and you can finish later.

Prefer to paste? Tap Paste receipt at the top of the receipt screen.

  1. Open the Aldi app and find your receipt. Press and hold on the text, choose Select all, then Copy.
  2. Come back here, press and hold in the big box and choose Paste. Tap Read receipt.
  3. The lines open in the table. Lines in red need you to tap the name and pick what the item is. If it isn't food, tap Not food.

You only have to choose an item once. Next time, the app remembers it.

Item numbers. Each Aldi product has a 6-digit number printed at the left of its receipt line. When a pasted line has one, the app matches by number first, and repeated lines of the same product become one line with a count. When you choose the item for a new number, the app remembers it. You can also type a number in the item search to find that item.

Paper receipt? Tap Scan a photo. The photo is read on your phone, then the lines open in the table for you to check.

What do I tap after I cook or eat?

On the Days tab, tap Cooked it under the meal you made. The app shows what it will take out of your kitchen. Tap Confirm if it looks right. Extra servings are saved as leftovers.

When you eat leftovers, tap Ate it. It takes the usual portions for that meal. Ate a different amount? Tap Some left, set it with − and +, and confirm.

Changed your plans? Tap Other, then Not tonight to move the meal to tomorrow or put it back on your menu. Tap Ate out if you ate something else. Tap Change to pick a different meal.

The app thinks I have food I don't have.

Tap At home, then Kitchen. Tap the item, then Amount. Choose about how much is left: Empty, ¼, ½, ¾ or Full. You can also use the − and + buttons.

Items marked Eat soon should be used in the next day or two. Red means the food is past its use-by date. Tap Freeze if you put something in the freezer, and the app gives it more time.

What are staples?

Staples are things that last a long time, like salt, spices, oil and flour. The app doesn't measure them. It only asks whether you have them.

On At home › Pantry, tap a staple to change it between Have, Low and Out. Anything marked Out goes on your shopping list.

I tapped the wrong thing. Can I undo it?

Yes. Tap the curved arrow at the top left of any screen to undo. The arrow next to it redoes.

Missed it? Press and hold the undo arrow, or open Settings › Help and about. The History list shows your recent changes. Tap Undo on the top one. You can keep tapping to go further back.

Something I'm buying isn't in the app.

On the Groceries tab, tap + Add item to cart. Search first. If it isn't there, tap Add as a new item.

Type the name and the shelf price, and pick a category, such as Dairy & eggs. The category tells the app how long it keeps and where it goes. It lands in your cart straight away.

To fix a name or price later, go to Settings, then My added items, and tap Edit.

A price looks wrong.

On the shopping list, tap the price, type the right one and tap Save.

Logging your receipt updates prices for you. If you paid much less than usual, the app asks whether it was a sale. A sale price doesn't change the usual price.

How do stores, groups and favorites work?

An item can be sold at several stores, each with its own price, size and item number. The shopping list uses the store where it costs least per ounce, pound or piece.

Items sit in groups, from general to exact: Meat › Chicken › Chicken wings › Boneless. Sort them in Settings › Item groups. When a cheaper item from the same group is sold somewhere, the list shows it in green under the item.

To pick a favorite, open an item (⋯ on the list, then Item details) and switch on My favorite. Recipes that ask for the general group use your favorite.

How does the Google Sheet work?

The Google Sheet is the master copy of prices, items and recipes. The app reads it when it opens.

Changes you make on the phone (new items, prices, recipes, receipt names, Aldi numbers) are sent to the sheet. Without a connection they wait on the phone and go out later.

If the same thing was changed in both places, the sheet wins and the app tells you. Connect it in Settings › Google Sheet.

How do I keep my information safe?

Everything you enter is saved only on this phone. Clearing your browser's data, or getting a new phone, can erase it.

Once a week, open Backup and restore in Settings and tap Save as file. The file goes to your phone's Downloads folder.

If your phone offers Share backup file, you can send it straight to Google Drive or your email.

To bring your information back, tap Restore from file and pick that file.

The app also keeps 5 automatic copies on this phone. They are listed under Automatic copies. They help if something goes wrong, but they don't survive clearing Chrome's data, so keep making your own backup file too.

The text is too small, or I want an icon on my home screen.

Bigger text: open your phone's Settings app, tap Display, then Font size, and slide it larger. This app follows that setting.

Home screen icon: open this app in Chrome, tap the three dots ⋮ in the top corner, then tap Add to Home screen. Always open the app from that icon so your information stays in one place.
